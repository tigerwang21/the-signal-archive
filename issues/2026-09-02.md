# Adoption Is Total. Provision Is Not.

#### Surrey embeds discipline-specific AI across every degree

From September 2026, AI teaching runs through all programmes — foundation year to postgraduate — with students interrogating AI outputs against disciplinary evidence. Curriculum-wide, discipline-tailored AI is becoming the benchmark; bolt-on modules signal lag.

[descriptionSOURCE: ACADEMICJOBS ↗](https://academicjobs.com/uk/higher-education-news/surrey-university-ai-degrees-redesign-2026-academicjobs-14703)

#### Fourteen research-intensive universities back DSIT's AI for Science Strategy

Oxford, Cambridge, UCL, King's, Bristol and Glasgow commit to specialist AI training, conversion courses and research fellowships — a clear signal of where UKRI and DSIT funding will flow.

[descriptionSOURCE: RESULTSENSE ↗](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-04-16-russell-group-universities-ai-science-strategy/)

#### AI-misuse sanctions tripled; four Russell Group universities expelled students

FOI data records 2,053 AI-related sanctions in 2024/25 — roughly one in 170 students — up from about 700. Enforcement is escalating unevenly while detection remains unreliable; registrars should quantify local exposure now.

[descriptionSOURCE: THE TAB ↗](https://thetab.com/2026/05/19/wow-these-four-russell-group-universities-actually-expelled-students-for-using-ai)

#### Georgia Tech publishes a faculty-authored AI strategy — a US benchmark

Backed by 67 faculty articles and 20 classroom lessons, it maps fluency-for-all, enterprise innovation and institutional leadership. A replicable model: survey what faculty already do, then codify it.

[descriptionSOURCE: GEORGIA TECH (US) ↗](https://news.research.gatech.edu/2026/08/31/academic-ai-strategy-establishes-shared-vision-teaching-and-learning-georgia-tech)

★ Featured · US

#### MIT urges "AI-aware" curricula and disclosure over bans

A faculty-staff-student committee rejects both shielding students from AI and "AI-proofing" the curriculum — steering and scaffolding AI use rather than prohibiting it. A governance benchmark with direct relevance to UK assessment-integrity debates.

[descriptionSOURCE: GOVTECH (US) ↗](https://govtech.com/education/higher-ed/mit-outlines-responsible-use-policy-recommendations-for-ai)

#### HEPI: 95% of undergraduates use AI, but support lags sharply

The third Student Generative AI Survey finds 95% use AI and 94% in assessed work — yet only 36% feel encouraged and 38% are given tools. Closing that provision gap is now the highest-ROI teaching intervention.

[descriptionSOURCE: HEPI ↗](https://www.hepi.ac.uk/reports/student-generative-ai-survey-2026/)

#### Frontier AI cannot yet grade essays reliably

Across 761 psychology essays, AI matched human degree classifications only 35–65% of the time, rewarding length and vocabulary over judgement — a strong UK evidence base to resist automating marking.

[descriptionSOURCE: PHYS.ORG ↗](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-ai-good-grade-university-essays.pdf)

#### Most AI policies are compliance instruments in educational language

HEPI's review of 96 institutions finds many "promise critical thinking but deliver surveillance." Policy quality is now a differentiator — audit whether yours builds critical literacy or defaults to detection.

[descriptionSOURCE: HEPI / ACADEMICJOBS ↗](https://academicjobs.com/higher-education-news/hepi-report-on-uk-university-ai-policies-critical-literacy-academicjobs-23116)

#### China mandates AI literacy as a public required course

The "AI + Education" Action Plan makes AI a compulsory foundational course at every university — 626 institutions now run AI majors and over 600,000 students are enrolled. A whole-system benchmark, though one to read alongside China's very different governance model.

[descriptionSOURCE: MOE CHINA ↗](https://academicjobs.com/higher-education-news/china-ai-education-plan-mandatory-university-course-academicjobs-12326)

#### UK graduate vacancies hit a decade low as AI automates entry-level work

Adzuna records just 8,383 graduate jobs in July — down 45% year-on-year — against a record 262,820 students entering this September. Graduate employability is now a structural AI risk.

[descriptionSOURCE: THE GUARDIAN / ADZUNA ↗](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/aug/24/uk-graduate-job-vacancies-plunge-adzuna)

#### Government scales free AI training to 10 million workers by 2030

The DfE-led AI Skills Boost extends free AI training across the workforce. A national upskilling push universities can anchor to through CPD, degree apprenticeships and employer partnerships.

[descriptionSOURCE: GOV.UK ↗](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/free-ai-training-for-all-as-government-and-industry-programme-expands-to-provide-10-million-workers-with-key-ai-skills-by-2030)

#### Faculty capability, not tools, is the binding constraint

Pearson/AWS finds only 13% of higher-ed leaders rate faculty AI knowledge "very strong." The highest-leverage investment is faculty upskilling, not tool procurement.

[descriptionSOURCE: PEARSON / AWS ↗](https://www.pearson.com/content/dam/global-store/global/resources/ai-readiness/AI-Readiness-Report-2026.pdf)

#### US: the skills mismatch costs $1.1 trillion a year

Wharton–Accenture finds workers oversupply generalist traits while employers seek specialised, execution-oriented capability — the same signalling gap the UK's Adzuna data exposes.

[descriptionSOURCE: WHARTON–ACCENTURE (US) ↗](https://www.accenture.com/content/dam/accenture/final/accenture-com/document-fy26/q3/2026-Wharton-Accenture-Report-FNL.pdf)

#### DSIT's call for evidence on data regulation closes 9 September

It seeks practical examples of where UK GDPR and the Data (Use and Access) Act break down under AI. A rare, imminent window to shape UK data law — respond now with examples from research, admissions or student analytics.

[descriptionSOURCE: GOV.UK ↗](https://www.gov.uk/government/calls-for-evidence/data-regulation-in-the-age-of-ai-and-other-data-intensive-technologies)

#### The Data (Use and Access) Act's data-protection provisions are now in force

Since 5 February, the new "recognised legitimate interest" basis, reworked DSAR rules, and a new deepfake offence apply. Refresh RoPAs, DPIAs and DSAR procedures — the ICO has flagged universities as particularly affected.

[descriptionSOURCE: DLA PIPER ↗](https://privacymatters.dlapiper.com/2026/02/uk-commencement-of-the-data-protection-provisions-in-the-data-use-and-access-act/)

#### DSIT is being dismantled and redistributed across Whitehall

Science and innovation move to a new department, digital regulation returns to DCMS, and a Cabinet-level AI minister oversees a new Cabinet Office AI Taskforce. Recalibrate which Whitehall interlocutors you engage.

[descriptionSOURCE: POLITICO ↗](https://www.politico.eu/article/the-rise-and-fall-of-the-uk-tech-department/)

#### EU AI Act: high-risk education obligations deferred to December 2027

The Digital Omnibus pushes back high-risk duties ~16 months, but prohibited practices and AI-literacy provisions already apply — relevant to UK institutions operating or recruiting in the EU.

[descriptionSOURCE: EU / CASRAI ↗](https://casrai.org/news/eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-deadline-delay)
